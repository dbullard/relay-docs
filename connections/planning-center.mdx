## What you can do

Relay can use Planning Center for:

- Service and plan selection
- Rundown display
- Team assignments
- Person modules linked to positions
- Embedded Services Live views

## Connect your account

1. Open `Relay > Settings`.
2. Go to `External Connections`.
3. Select `Planning Center`.
4. Click `Connect to Planning Center`.
5. Finish sign-in and approval in your browser.
6. Return to Relay and confirm the connection is active.

<Note>If Planning Center modules say setup is required, it usually means the account is connected but the active tab is not configured yet.</Note>

## Configure the active tab

Connecting your Planning Center account is only the first step. The current tab also needs its own planning context.

On the tab you want to use:

1. Open tab settings.
2. Choose the service type.
3. Choose the plan.
4. Choose the service time.
5. Configure any relevant team settings.
6. Save the tab settings.

## What Planning Center modules can show

Depending on your setup, Relay can display:

- Plan information
- Service rundown items
- Service time details
- Team assignments
- Team member status
- Embedded Services Live content

## Person modules and assignments

Person modules can be filled manually or linked to Planning Center positions.

Typical setup:

1. Add a Person module.
2. Set the source to a Planning Center team.
3. Choose the team and position.
4. Pick an assignment when more than one person is available.

If your team uses headshots, Relay can often match image files by name when a headshot folder is configured in general settings.

## Troubleshooting

### The module says setup is required

Check both of these:

- Planning Center is connected in settings
- The active tab has a service, plan, and service time selected

### No plan items appear

Check whether the selected plan actually contains items for the selected service time.

### Team assignments are missing

Check whether:

- Team settings are configured on the tab
- The selected plan contains assigned people
- The Person module is pointed at the correct team and position
