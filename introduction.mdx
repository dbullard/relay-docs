Relay is a Mac and iPad app that lets live production teams build custom dashboards for events, services, and shows. You arrange tiles on a flexible canvas, load each tile with modules like Planning Center, ProPresenter, timers, wireless devices, audio meters, or live transcription, and then switch into Live Mode when it is time to run the production.

## What is included

- **Canvas editor**: Arrange, resize, align, and group tiles on a flexible canvas.
- **Modules**: Show live or static information from planning, presentation, audio, wireless, and utility workflows.
- **Live Mode**: Reduce accidental edits and activate live-focused dashboard behavior during an event.
- **Automation View**: Connect triggers, actions, and destinations without writing code.
- **LAN sharing**: Mirror a tab read-only, keep it live-synced and editable across devices with Sync, or share a browser view-only link on your local network.
- **OSC and MIDI**: Send and receive control messages for network and show-control workflows.
- **Relay Pro**: Unlock advanced modules and workflows through your license or subscription.
