## Export logs

Relay can export logs and diagnostic information to help investigate issues.

To export logs:

1. Open Relay.
2. Choose `Help > Export Logs`.
3. Save the log archive.
4. Share it with support if requested.

You can also find these tools in `Relay > Settings > Help`.

## Copy diagnostic summary

If you need a faster support-friendly snapshot:

1. Open `Relay > Settings > Help`.
2. Click `Copy Diagnostic Summary`.
3. Paste the result into your support message.

The diagnostic summary is often the fastest way to share current state without sending a full archive first.

## What logs may include

Support logs can include:

- Connection endpoints
- Device names
- LAN activity
- OSC paths
- Wireless diagnostics
- App errors
- Update-related details

<Warning>Review logs before sharing if your environment includes sensitive device names, addresses, or other production details.</Warning>

## Permissions to review

Relay may need several macOS permissions depending on the features you use.

### Microphone

Needed for SPL Meter and microphone-based transcription workflows.

### Speech Recognition

Needed for Live Transcription.

### Local Network

Needed for:

- Device discovery
- LAN sharing
- ProPresenter discovery
- OSC and MIDI network workflows
- Other networked integrations

### Screen or system audio capture

May be needed when system audio transcription workflows are enabled.

## Where to check permissions

1. Open `Relay > Settings`.
2. Select `Access`.
3. Review the current permission states.

You can also review them in macOS under `System Settings > Privacy & Security`.

## Troubleshooting

### I changed a permission but Relay still acts like it is missing

Quit and reopen Relay, then test again.

### I am not sure whether I should send logs or only the summary

Start with the diagnostic summary when support needs a quick overview. Send the full log archive when support asks for deeper investigation.
