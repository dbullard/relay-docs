## Manage your plan

Relay uses a license or subscription to unlock Pro features.

To review your current state:

1. Open `Relay > Settings`.
2. Go to the license or subscription area.
3. Confirm whether Relay shows Pro access or Free plan access.

Common tasks from that screen may include:

- Activating a license
- Refreshing license status
- Managing a subscription
- Deactivating a license on the current Mac

## Updates

To check for updates:

1. Open Relay.
2. Choose `Relay > Check for Updates` if available.
3. Follow the prompts to install the update.

Relay may also use different update behavior for beta builds or beta feeds depending on configuration.

## Troubleshooting

### Pro features still look locked

Check:

- The Mac is online
- The license was activated successfully
- License status was refreshed after purchase or renewal

### Updating fails

Check:

- Relay is installed in the Applications folder
- The app is not still being run from a disk image
- Logs were exported if the update keeps failing
