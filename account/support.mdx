If something is not working as expected in Relay, start with the troubleshooting guidance below and the diagnostic tools built into the app.

## Export logs

1. Choose `Help > Export Logs...`.
2. Save the archive.
3. Share it with support if requested.

You can also find log tools in `Relay > Settings > Help`.

## Copy a diagnostic summary

Open `Relay > Settings > Help` and click `Copy Diagnostic Summary` to get a quick text snapshot of current state.

## Common troubleshooting tips

- Quit and reopen Relay
- Check permissions in `Relay > Settings > Access`
- Refresh your license
- Check for updates
- Confirm the network path to the connected device or app

## Contact support

If you still need help, contact `support@ondeckapps.com`.

Include:

- what you expected to happen
- what actually happened
- app version
- screenshots if helpful
- diagnostic summary or exported logs when available
