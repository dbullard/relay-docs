Relay for Mac uses a license or subscription to unlock Pro features.

## Review your current status

1. Open `Relay > Settings`.
2. Go to the license or subscription page.
3. Confirm whether Relay shows Free plan access or Pro access.

## Common tasks

- Activate a license
- Refresh license status
- Manage a subscription
- Deactivate a license on the current Mac

## Troubleshooting

If Pro features remain locked, confirm the Mac is online and refresh the current license status after purchase or renewal.
