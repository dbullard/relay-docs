Relay provides several utility modules that help you structure your dashboard, display static information, and execute simple commands without needing a full integration.

## Informational Modules

These modules let you present static content or embedded media directly on your canvas.

- **Text:** Display static labels, notes, or instructions. You can use this to label sections of your dashboard or provide cues to operators.
- **Image:** Load custom logos or graphics into a tile. Useful for branding your dashboard or displaying reference images.
- **Document:** Provides a preview-only view of a PDF or Word document. Perfect for keeping rundowns, stage plots, or schedules visible without leaving the app.
- **Webpage:** Embed a live web view. This is useful for displaying external resources, dashboards, or web-based tools alongside your Relay modules.

## Structural and Action Modules

These modules help you organize your tile layout and execute tasks.

- **Separator:** Adds a visual break (horizontal or vertical line) between other modules within the same tile, helping you group related controls visually.
- **Button:** A simple push-button that can trigger in-app actions, like clearing transcriptions or starting SPL logging. Buttons can also be bound to trigger nodes in Automation View to execute complex workflows manually.
- **Picker:** Used in conjunction with Rosters, this module lets you assign people or equipment to specific roles dynamically.

## Time and Clocks

Keeping your team on schedule is critical in live production.

- **Time:** Displays the current system time in a highly visible format.
- **Timecode:** Monitors and displays incoming MIDI or OSC timecode data, ensuring operators stay synchronized with your timeline.
