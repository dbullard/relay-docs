Trigger nodes are the starting points of your automation graphs. When a trigger condition is met, a signal is sent down the graph to execute actions.

## App State Trigger
Fires when specific application events occur, such as when Relay launches, when Live Mode is toggled on or off, or when you switch tabs.

## PCO Plan Updated
Triggers when your connected Planning Center plan changes. You can use the live item picker to filter for specific item changes (e.g., when a specific song starts).

## Time
Triggers at a specific time of day or based on a recurring interval.

## dB Threshold
Fires when an audio input (measured by an SPL Meter or Global Measurement input) crosses a specific decibel threshold. Useful for auto-muting or triggering visual alerts.

## Manual Trigger
A trigger that fires when you manually click a bound Button module on your dashboard.
