Actions are the endpoints of your automation graphs. They receive signals from triggers and execute commands.

## System & LAN Notifications
- **System Notification:** Displays a standard macOS notification banner.
- **LAN Notification:** Broadcasts a notification alert to other Relay instances connected on your local network.

## HTTP Request
Triggers any web endpoint or API. Useful for integrating with custom services, triggering webhooks, or controlling smart home devices.

## ProPresenter Action
Triggers specific actions in ProPresenter, such as firing a specific slide, clearing layers, or starting a timer.

## Protocol Sends
- **OSC Send:** Transmits an Open Sound Control message to a specific IP and port.
- **MIDI Send:** Sends a MIDI note or control change message to a connected MIDI destination.

## Console & Wireless Actions
Send specific commands to connected audio consoles or wireless device receivers (e.g., muting a channel or recalling a snapshot).
