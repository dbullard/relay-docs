<Warning>The YouTube Live Stream Monitor is currently a beta feature introduced in Relay 1.1.1.</Warning>

The YouTube Live Stream Monitor module lets you keep an eye on your live broadcasts directly from your dashboard, ensuring your stream is healthy and tracking viewer engagement.

## Setup

1. Add a **YouTube Live Stream Monitor** module to a tile.
2. In the inspector, authenticate Relay with your Google/YouTube account.
3. Once authenticated, use the broadcast picker to select your active or upcoming stream.

## Features

- **Live Statistics:** View real-time metrics including concurrent viewers, total likes, and chat activity.
- **Stream Health:** Monitor the incoming video bitrate and overall connection quality to ensure your stream doesn't drop.
- **Fast Loading:** The broadcast picker is optimized to load results quickly and automatically filters out streams that have already ended.
- **Persistence:** Your previously selected broadcast will remain in the picker even if it momentarily drops from the active results list.

## Troubleshooting

- If the module fails to load data, double-check that your Google account authorization hasn't expired.
- Ensure your Mac has an active internet connection to poll the YouTube API.
- Since this is a beta feature, you may encounter occasional layout or connection quirks. Please report any consistent issues through the Help menu!
