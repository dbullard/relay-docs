Keep an eye on your visual elements and presentation software directly from your dashboard.

## ProPresenter
The ProPresenter module displays current and next slide text, timers, and stage messages. It connects directly to your ProPresenter instance over the local network.

## NDI Feed [BETA]
The NDI Feed module brings low-latency video monitoring to your dashboard by capturing NDI sources on your network. This is a beta-sensitive module; always validate its performance on your actual production network.
