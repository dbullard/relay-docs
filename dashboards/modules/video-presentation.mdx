Keep an eye on your visual elements and presentation software directly from your dashboard.

## ProPresenter

The ProPresenter module connects directly to your ProPresenter instance over the local network to display presentation data.

### Setup
1. Enable the ProPresenter Network API within ProPresenter's settings.
2. Add the **ProPresenter** module to a Relay tile.
3. Enter the IP address and port of your ProPresenter machine.

### Features
- **Slide Text:** View the current and next slide text directly on your dashboard.
- **Stage Messages:** Monitor active stage messages being sent to the platform.
- **Timers:** Keep track of active ProPresenter clocks and segment timers.

## NDI Feed [BETA]

The NDI Feed module brings low-latency video monitoring to your dashboard by capturing NDI sources on your network.

### Setup
1. Ensure your Mac is on the same local network as your NDI sources.
2. Add the **NDI Feed** module to a tile.
3. Select the desired NDI stream from the dropdown list.

### Features
- **Low Latency:** Monitor cameras or presentation outputs with minimal delay.
- **Bandwidth Management:** Adjust the feed quality if you are monitoring multiple streams to save network bandwidth.
- **Beta Warning:** Always validate performance on your actual production network, as high NDI traffic can impact Wi-Fi stability.

## YouTube Live Stream Monitor [BETA]

Keep an eye on your live broadcasts directly from your dashboard, ensuring your stream is healthy and tracking viewer engagement.

### Setup
1. Add a **YouTube Live Stream Monitor** module to a tile.
2. In the inspector, authenticate Relay with your Google/YouTube account.
3. Once authenticated, use the broadcast picker to select your active or upcoming stream.

### Features
- **Live Statistics:** View real-time metrics including concurrent viewers, total likes, and chat activity.
- **Stream Health:** Monitor the incoming video bitrate and overall connection quality to ensure your stream doesn't drop.
- **Fast Loading:** The broadcast picker is optimized to load results quickly and automatically filters out streams that have already ended.
