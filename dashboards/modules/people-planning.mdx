These modules help you manage people, roles, and service plans directly from your dashboard.

## Planning Center
The Planning Center module connects to your PCO account to display service rundowns. It updates automatically as your plans change, ensuring operators always see the latest schedule.

## PCO Team Assignments
This module surfaces the people scheduled in Planning Center for specific roles. It provides a visual breakdown of who is serving and their confirmation status.

## Roster Team Assignments
If you aren't using Planning Center, Relay's built-in Rosters let you build your own teams locally. This module displays assignments based on your local Roster database.

## Person
The Person module is a flexible tile that displays a specific role or individual. It can be dynamically assigned using the Picker module or updated via automation workflows.
