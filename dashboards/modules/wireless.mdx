Monitor the health of your wireless audio systems, including RF strength, battery life, and audio levels.

<Warning>BETA FEATURES</Warning>

All wireless modules are currently beta-sensitive or restricted to beta builds. Test them in your actual environment before relying on them live.

## Shure Overview [BETA]
A centralized module for monitoring multiple Shure wireless devices at once. It uses dynamic polling tiers to efficiently surface warning states, low batteries, or offline channels without needing individual tiles for every mic.

## Shure [BETA]
A detailed module for monitoring a single Shure wireless channel, showing granular RF, battery, audio, and transmitter reporting. 

## Sennheiser [BETA]
Provides monitoring for Sennheiser wireless systems. *Note: This module is currently only available in beta or debug builds.*
