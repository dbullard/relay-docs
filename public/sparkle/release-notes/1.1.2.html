What's New in Relay

New Features

  • Real-Time Audio Analyzer: See your audio levels in real-time with a detailed frequency analyzer. Perfect for monitoring microphones and audio inputs during services.
  • Audio Spectrograph: Visual representation of your audio across different frequencies, displayed as a scrolling waterfall for easy monitoring over time.
  • SPL Logging: Automatically record audio level data to files for later review and analysis. Start and stop recording with a single tap.
  • Per-Input Audio Settings: Fine-tune calibration for each individual audio input from a dedicated settings panel.

Improvements

  • Audio level calculations are now more accurate and responsive.
  • Settings changes take effect immediately without needing to restart.
  • Better handling of multiple audio inputs simultaneously.
  • Smoother performance when working with audio monitoring.
  • More reliable connection handling for wireless microphone systems.

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed audio level display showing incorrect values in some cases.
  • Fixed calibration settings being applied twice to audio levels.
  • Fixed crashes when switching between audio analysis views.
  • Fixed audio recording crashes with certain audio formats.
  • Reduced unnecessary error reports from wireless microphone connections.
  • Fixed Smart Light device authentication.
  • Improved overall stability and reliability.
