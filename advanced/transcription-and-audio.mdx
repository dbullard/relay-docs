## Live Transcription

Live Transcription converts a selected audio input into text that can appear on the dashboard.

Typical setup:

1. Add a Live Transcription module to a tile.
2. Choose the input source from your Global Audio Settings.
3. Review quality or behavior settings.
4. Enter Live Mode to run transcription.

Depending on your Mac and permissions, Relay may be able to use microphone input and supported system audio capture paths.

Useful transcript tools can include:

- Showing live transcript entries
- Copying the transcript
- Exporting transcript text
- Clearing the transcript
- Jumping to the most recent entry

Live Transcription can also support word-trigger automation workflows.

### Advanced Transcription Controls

Transcription inputs are managed centrally under the **Transcription** tab of your **Global Audio Settings**. From there, you can configure:

- Hardware device and specific channel selection.
- **Auto-gain sensitivity** and engine preference per-input.
- **Word Substitutions** to easily filter or replace specific words on the fly.
- **Profanity Controls** to automatically mask or remove profanity.

## Global Audio Settings

Relay uses a centralized configuration manager for all your audio sources to streamline setup across the app. It's split into two main tabs:
- **Measurement:** Configure inputs used for visual monitoring like the SPL Meter, RTA, and Spectrograph. Here you can add inputs, set a calibration offset (in dB), and choose a custom display color for visualization graphs.
- **Transcription:** Configure inputs specifically used for speech-to-text as detailed above.

## SPL Meter

The SPL Meter module displays sound level data from a Mac audio input.

Typical setup:

1. Add an SPL Meter module.
2. Choose a Measurement input configured in your Global Audio Settings.
3. Select weighting and response settings.
4. Enter Live Mode when your workflow expects live updates.

Common options include:

- A, C, or flat weighting
- Fast or slow response
- Calibration using a known reference
- Logging SPL history in Live Mode. Relay can record SPL data directly to CSV files, showing a pulsing green indicator on your tiles when recording is active.

<Warning>If transcription does not start or SPL data looks frozen, check permissions before changing the whole dashboard. The issue is often at the macOS level.</Warning>

## Real-Time Analyzer (RTA) & Spectrograph

For deeper audio monitoring, Relay offers visual analysis modules that pull directly from your Measurement inputs in Global Audio Settings:

- **Real-Time Analyzer (RTA):** View frequency bands of your audio inputs in real-time with bar and filled-curve display modes, plus peak-hold capabilities. Analyzer resolution can be adjusted between 1-octave to 1/48-octave bands for highly detailed acoustic monitoring.
- **Spectrograph:** A scrolling waterfall view for visualizing audio frequency distributions over time. The display automatically utilizes the custom color you defined for that input in your global settings.

These tools are perfect for monitoring microphones and checking audio characteristics live during services.

## Permissions that matter

These tools commonly depend on:

- Microphone access
- Speech Recognition access
- A usable audio input source

If system audio transcription is part of the workflow, screen or system-audio capture permissions may also matter depending on the capture path.

## Troubleshooting

### Transcription does not start

Check:

- Microphone permission
- Speech Recognition permission
- Whether another app is already using the same input
- Whether Live Mode is required for your current setup

### SPL Meter is paused or flat

Check:

- The selected input source
- Microphone permission
- Whether Live Mode is enabled
- Whether calibration assumptions are reasonable
