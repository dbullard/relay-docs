Relay supports wireless monitoring workflows so your dashboard can show channel status, battery, RF, and related live information.

## Shure workflows

Shure devices can be added manually or discovered on the network. Once configured, modules can show:

- Device name
- Channel name
- Frequency
- Battery
- RF and audio information

## Beta and debug-only wireless areas

Some wireless features are beta-sensitive:

- Sennheiser module availability in beta or debug builds
- beta-only Shure models such as `ANX4`, `ADTD`, and `ADTQ`
- verbose wireless diagnostics controls in the Beta settings page

## Automation tie-ins

Wireless systems can feed trigger conditions and destination commands in Automation View depending on the device and build.

## Troubleshooting

If wireless data is missing, confirm the device is added, reachable, selected in the module, and available in the current build type.
